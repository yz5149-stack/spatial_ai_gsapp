        How it answers the brief

        Semantic model

        A creature, survivors, a refuge, warm places, caches, streets, buildings, terrain and a storm, with their relations: see semantic-model.md.

        Two notions of location

        Every position is held in several frames: the grid cell, metres east and south of the map's corner, longitude and latitude (WGS84), and the street ("Amsterdam Av, near W 113th St"). Landmarks are stored relative to the previous landmark, in metres.

        Two notions of distance

        Euclidean (metres, also in 3D with the terrain), walking distance on the agent's own map, acoustic distance (straight line + muffling by buildings and trees, carried downwind), and effort/cold: the route cost the survivors plan with (snow depth, wind exposure).

        Two kinds of agents

        The creature (explore, then hunt by sound) and the survivors (several at once, each with warmth, stamina and battery). Both see only a cone, blocked by buildings, trees and the rising ground; both build their own map and landmarks.

        Memory, goals, performance, planning

        The creature patrols the landmarks where it has heard or caught survivors before. Survivors remember warm places and caches, and before setting out for the refuge they estimate the heat they will lose on the way; if it is too much, they warm up first.

        The environment

        Physical, partially observable, multi-agent (survivors never share a cell), dynamic (the weather and the snow change every hour) and non-deterministic (where survivors wake, how they search).

        Obstacles, hindrances, affordances

        Obstacles: buildings, trees, fences, water, barricades. Hindrances: deep snow, wind exposure, slopes and steps, street light (you are seen). Affordances: warming centres, subway air, shop doorways, caches, plowed streets, the lee of buildings.

        Real data, 3D + time

        OpenStreetMap streets and parks; NYC building footprints with surveyed roof heights and ground elevations; Open-Meteo hourly temperature, wind, gusts, snowfall and snow depth for the storm; a DEM for the parks. Or tonight's live weather.
