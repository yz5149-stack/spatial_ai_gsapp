The real place

125th Street and Amsterdam Avenue, Harlem: about 650 × 700 m from Broadway to Morningside Avenue, W 122nd to W 129th Street, with the General Grant Houses and the 125th Street shops. Buildings are the city's surveyed footprints and roof heights; the ground follows its surveyed elevation points; the simulation runs on 6 m cells.

How it works

  1. Trash goes out. Each building sets out bags at the rule's hour; restaurants after closing. Bags tear open hour by hour; bins keep almost everything in.
  2. Rats forage. They smell food along the open air (about 100 m), run along walls, avoid light and people, and remember where and when they ate, and where they were chased.
  3. The patrol hunts. Inspectors answer 311 calls with flashlights. A rat in the beam is chased: caught, or it gets away — into its burrow, which gives the burrow away.
  4. You design. Ask "what if?": tick changes, run a week, compare with today. Or let the computer vary and evolve streets.
