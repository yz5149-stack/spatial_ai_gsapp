    Rat City

    Real streets, buildings and trash schedules of Morningside Heights and West Harlem, on Google's 3D city. Rats come out after dark: they smell food along the open air, run along walls, keep out of light and away from people, and remember where they ate. Inspectors come by day: they answer 311 calls, look for droppings and gnawing, follow the trail to the burrow, bait it and seal it.

    The colonies start where the city's own inspectors found rats in the last three years. You can drop a trash bag and watch them come, compare the rules of 2022 with today's, and design the street yourself.

    3D tiles: Google, via Cesium ion. Data: NYC Open Data (311, DOHMH rodent and restaurant inspections, DOB shed permits, DSNY baskets, building footprints, elevation), MTA ridership, OpenStreetMap.
