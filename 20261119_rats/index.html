    The real place

    125th Street and Amsterdam Avenue, Harlem: about 650 × 700 m between Broadway and Morningside Avenue, W 122nd to W 129th Street, with the General Grant Houses and the 125th Street shops. The point cloud is generated from the city's survey: every building footprint and roof height, every ground elevation point, every street tree; the simulation runs on 6 m cells.

    HOW IT WORKS

    1. Trash goes out. Each building sets out bags at the rule's hour; restaurants after closing. Bags tear open hour by hour; bins keep almost everything out of reach.
    2. Rats forage. They smell food along the open air (about 100 m), run along walls, avoid light and people, remember where and when they ate, and where they were chased.
    3. The patrol hunts. Inspectors answer 311 calls with flashlights. A rat in the beam is chased: caught, or it gets away — into its burrow, which gives the burrow away.
    4. You design. Change rules and the street; compare two variations or let designs evolve over a week of simulated nights.
